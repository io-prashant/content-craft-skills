# AI Tools & News Radar

## Three questions before anything gets covered

1. **Did you see it on the primary source?** The company's own announcement, the repo, the official site. A screenshot of someone else's tweet is not a source.
2. **Can the audience actually try it?** If it demands a paid API key, a credit card, or serious hardware, that goes on the slide - no silent catches.
3. **Is it explained rather than hyped?** What it does, who it serves, one genuine use, one honest limitation. Words like "best", "game changer" and "insane" are not substance.

## Where to look (in order)

**Primary announcements first:**

- OpenAI: https://openai.com/news/
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/news
- Google DeepMind: https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/
- Meta AI: https://ai.meta.com/blog/
- DeepSeek: https://github.com/deepseek-ai
- Moonshot AI (Kimi): https://www.moonshot.ai/ and https://github.com/MoonshotAI
- Microsoft and Google AI skilling repos on GitHub

**Daily digests second** - leads only, and every lead gets re-verified against a primary source before publication:

- The Rundown AI: https://www.therundown.ai/
- Future Tools: https://futuretools.io/

**Educators and creators third** - good for angles and formats; verify every claim yourself.

## Free prompt collections

- prompts.chat (formerly f/awesome-chatgpt-prompts), community-maintained: https://github.com/f/awesome-chatgpt-prompts
- DAIR.AI's Prompt Engineering Guide - prompting, RAG and agents: https://github.com/dair-ai/prompt-engineering-guide

## The real-cost audit

"Free to download" says nothing about "free to use". Before recommending any tool or repo, check and state all five:

- **License** - MIT/Apache/CC0 means reuse-friendly; anything else means read the terms.
- **Runtime cost** - free tier, local-only, or metered API keys?
- **Hardware** - an ordinary laptop, or a GPU?
- **Setup** - can a beginner clone and run, or does it need Docker and accounts?
- **Maintenance** - commits within recent months, or abandoned?

## From finding to post

1. One finding per post - no roundup dumps.
2. Lead with what the audience can do with it today.
3. Show the real tool running (screenshot or short recording). Never dress a mockup up as the real thing.
4. Prompt posts: full copy-paste prompts with a filled example.
5. News posts: what happened, why the audience should care, what to ignore. No fear framing, no "AI is coming for your job" bait.
6. Credit sources in the caption. A claim that cannot be sourced gets cut, not softened.

## Worth covering? Score it (out of 100)

- Primary-source verified (25)
- Usable by the audience at a stated cost (20)
- Obvious use today (20)
- Limitations stated plainly (15)
- Source links ready for the caption (10)
- A fresh angle you have not covered lately (10)

Under 80: park it or drop it. Quota is never a reason to publish filler.
